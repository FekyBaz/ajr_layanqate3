# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive open-source community.

Expected behavior:

- Be respectful and professional.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination, and personal attacks.
- Respect the project's religious context while keeping technical discussion constructive.

Unacceptable behavior may result in removal from project spaces or other appropriate moderation actions.
