## Prototype Goal
Test whether the brand can hold emotional truth through minimal Arabic kinetic typography with almost no visual drama.

## Format
- 9:16
- 17.5 seconds
- text-first
- warm cream / parchment palette
- one tiny UI glimpse only

## Animation Intent
- typing cursor, not flashy kinetic type
- slow fades between states
- almost-static framing
- minimal texture movement only

## Review Questions
- Does it still feel sincere with no sound?
- Do the pauses feel thoughtful, not slow for effect?
- Does the UI glimpse feel like part of the thought, not product insertion?
- Does the final line land softly?
