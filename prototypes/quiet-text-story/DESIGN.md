## Style Prompt
Quiet digital spiritual reflection. A warm, vertical text-story video that feels like thoughts being typed into a calm space. The composition should resemble a modern minimal product poem, not an advertisement. Arabic typography is the hero. Motion is sparse, soft, and almost invisible. The emotional effect should come from pauses, spacing, and honesty rather than cinematic flourish.

## Colors
- `#f6f0e4` background
- `#fbf7ef` soft surface
- `#2f261b` primary text
- `#5c4d3b` secondary text
- `#8c6a35` accent

## Typography
- `Cairo` for main display text
- `Noto Naskh Arabic` for small supporting UI text

## What NOT to Do
- No glowing text or halos
- No oversized slogan energy
- No dramatic transitions or fast movement
- No sacred motifs used as decoration
- No hype, CTA pressure, or emotional soundtrack manipulation
