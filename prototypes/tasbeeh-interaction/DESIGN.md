## Style Prompt
Warm, quiet, Arabic-first devotional interface. The scene should feel like a private morning worship moment on a phone, not an advertisement and not a cinematic spectacle. Use soft cream, muted gold, and grounded brown tones taken from the product UI. Motion should be minimal and human-scale. Nothing should glow, shimmer, celebrate, or announce itself loudly.

## Colors
- `#f6f0e4` background canvas
- `#fffaf1` surface card
- `#8c6a35` primary accent
- `#2f261b` primary text
- `#5c4d3b` secondary text

## Typography
- `Cairo` for display labels and numbers
- `Noto Naskh Arabic` for supporting Arabic text

## What NOT to Do
- No sacred glow, particles, halos, or mystical lighting
- No luxury-product polish or glossy app reveal behavior
- No dramatic camera simulation, fast transitions, or trailer pacing
- No emotionally loaded music dependence
- No symbolic Islamic decoration added just to make the scene feel religious
