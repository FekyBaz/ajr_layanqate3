## Prototype
Prototype Scene 3: Tasbeeh Interaction

### Goal
Test whether the tasbeeh interface can become visual without becoming manipulative, over-cinematic, or spiritually artificial.

### Duration
6.5 seconds

### Format
9:16 vertical

### Audio
Deliberately omitted in the render package. The scene should remain sincere with no soundtrack. If audio is added later, it should be room tone plus one soft tap only.

### Timing
- `0.0 - 0.8s` settle into the phone surface
- `0.8 - 2.2s` calm stillness
- `2.2 - 2.6s` one tap interaction
- `2.6 - 6.5s` quiet hold on the updated count

### Validation intent
- should still work muted
- should still work if motion is reduced further
- should not feel like an ad or product promo
